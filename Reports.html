Transparency Reports

Transparency is at the heart of Them Cameo Box. Each month, we publish a brief report detailing our spending, progress, and projects so you can see exactly how your support fuels creativity on campus.

We believe our customers deserve to know where their costumes come from, how their money is used, and how we uphold our promise of ethical, accessible, and sustainable costuming.

These updates reflect our ongoing mission to make costuming simple, affordable, and community-driven. Will have links to each month, styled like a blog or cooking recipe hub
